# Corporate Governance Risk Score Methodology

## Objective
Create a transparent comparative screening model for governance-related risk across Nifty 100 companies.

## Components

### 1. Promoter pledge — 30 points
- 0%: 30
- >0% to 10%: 20
- >10% to 25%: 10
- >25%: 0

### 2. Auditor changes — 20 points
- 0 changes: 20
- 1 change: 10
- More than 1 or an adverse resignation context: 0

### 3. Related-party transactions — 20 points
- Low: 20
- Medium: 10
- High: 0

### 4. Independent directors — 15 points
- 50% or more: 15
- 33% to 49.99%: 10
- Below 33%: 0

### 5. ESG / BRSR disclosure — 15 points
- High: 15
- Medium: 8
- Low: 0

## Risk bands

| Total | Classification |
|---:|---|
| 80–100 | Low Risk |
| 60–79 | Moderate Risk |
| 40–59 | High Risk |
| 0–39 | Very High Risk |

## Research controls

All company-level inputs should be traceable to public filings, including shareholding patterns, annual reports, auditor notices, related-party disclosures, board composition, BRSR/BRSR Core disclosures and relevant NSE/BSE filings.

## Limitations

This is a user-defined screening model, not an official NSE rating, SEBI rating, credit rating or investment recommendation. Qualitative inputs such as RPT risk and ESG disclosure level require consistent analyst judgment and documented evidence.
